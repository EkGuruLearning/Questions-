## Hal (explanation)

- **Q1 (3) 96** — har number pichle ka dugna: 48 × 2 = 96.
- **Q2 (1) 4-15-7** — CAT = C(3), A(1), T(20). Isi tarah DOG = D(4), O(15), G(7).
- **Q3 (2) Rungs** — jis tarah pustak ke panne hote hain, usi tarah seedhi ke dande (rungs) hote hain.
- **Q4 (4) 42** — 16, 25 aur 36 pure varg hain (4², 5², 6²), 42 varg nahi hai.
- **Q5 (1) 5 km** — 3 aur 4 ke beech samkon (90°) hai, isliye seedhi doori = √(3² + 4²) = 5 km.
- **Q6 (1) Putri** — Q, P ki santan hai; R, Q ki behen hai, isliye R bhi P ki putri hai.
- **Q7 (3) 29** — 11, 13, 17, 19, 23 lagatar abhajya sankhyayen hain; agla prime 29 hai.
- **Q8 (2) 14va** — dayein se sthan = kul + 1 – baayein se sthan = 20 + 1 – 7 = 14.
- **Q9 (1) School** — jis tarah doctor hospital me kaam karta hai, teacher school me.
- **Q10 (1) TVOEBZ** — har akshar ek aage badhta hai: S→T, U→V, N→O, D→E, A→B, Y→Z.
