# Reasoning — Series (नमूना अध्याय)

## 1. Number series kaise kaam karti hai

Series me numbers ek hi niyam (pattern) se chalte rehte hain. Jawab dhoondhne ka tareeka:

- Pehle do padosi numbers ka **antar (difference)** dekho — 2, 6, 12, 20 me antar 4, 6, 8 hai.
- Antar barabar na ho to **antar ka antar** dekho — yahan 4, 6, 8 har baar 2 badh raha hai.
- Antar me pattern na mile to **guna/bhaag** dekho — 3, 6, 12, 24 har baar ×2 hai.
- Varg (1, 4, 9, 16 …), ghan (1, 8, 27, 64 …), abhajya sankhya (2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13 …) aur Fibonacci (1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8 …) bhi aksar pooche jaate hain.

## 2. Letter series aur varnmala

Angrezi varnmala me har letter ka ek number hota hai: A = 1, B = 2, … Z = 26. Letter series me aksar +2, +3, +4 … ya –1, –2, –3 … ka pattern hota hai. Ulti series bhi aati hai, jaise Z, X, V, T (har baar –2). Yaad rakhne wale jod: **EJOTY** — E = 5, J = 10, O = 15, T = 20, Y = 25.

## 3. Solved examples

Example 1 — 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
Antar: 4, 6, 8, 10 — har baar 2 badh raha hai. Agla antar 12 hoga, isliye jawab 30 + 12 = 42.

Example 2 — 3, 6, 12, 24, ?
Har number pichle number ka dugna hai (×2). Isliye 24 × 2 = 48.

Example 3 — 1, 4, 9, 16, ?
Ye 1², 2², 3², 4² hain. Agla 5² = 25.

Example 4 — 8, 27, 64, 125, ?
Ye 2³, 3³, 4³, 5³ hain. Agla 6³ = 216.

Example 5 — A, C, F, J, O, ?
A(1) se C(3) = +2, C se F = +3, F se J = +4, J se O = +5. Agla +6 hoga: O(15) + 6 = 21 = U.

Example 6 — 11, 13, 17, 19, 23, ?
Ye lagatar abhajya sankhyayen (prime numbers) hain. 23 ke baad agla prime 29 hai.

- Jawab hamesha options se milao — kabhi-kabhi do pattern bante hain, par options me sirf ek hi lagta hai.
- Time bachao: pehla antar dekh kar pattern mil gaya to aage na socho.
