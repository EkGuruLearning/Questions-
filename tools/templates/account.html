Private by design

Aapka data, aapka control.

  • Encrypted HttpOnly + Secure session cookies; login tokens JavaScript/localStorage mein nahi.
  • Session cookie up to 30 din; security settings ke hisaab se dobara login lag sakta hai.
  • Har subject/test supported. 200-result auto-delete nahi; 50-at-a-time paging aur export.
  • Offline results tab tak queued rehte hain jab tak sync na ho; purani guest history silently upload nahi hoti.

Device history account-filtered hai, encrypted vault nahi. Shared browser par logout aur zarurat par site data clear karein. Browser storage clear/evict ho sakti hai: important results export karein. Provider storage/inactivity quotas apply; lifetime-free availability guaranteed nahi.
