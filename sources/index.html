How to read the labels

Official-paper/key checked

Only a stable question mapped to an official paper and final/corrected official key qualifies. The current CN evidence register is linked above.

Answer checked

Answer-level curation only; it is not evidence of an official exam appearance or official answer-key match.

Claimed PYQ

A claim or source label in the repository remains unverified until matched to the relevant paper and key.

Original/internal practice

Practice, textbook paraphrase and repository-origin material are not relabelled as official questions.
